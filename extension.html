Pinned for Chrome

Install in 4 steps

Download the extension (.zip)

  1. Unzip the download. You will get a folder called pinned-extension.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the pinned-extension folder.
  4. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome and pin Pinned to your toolbar.

Heads up: the extension keeps its notes in this browser only. They do not sync with your website account.
